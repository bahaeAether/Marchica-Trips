About this activity

in the morning, Marchica lagoon is all yours: no wind, no noise, only the splash of the paddle and the changing light. Your guide shows you the essential strokes in ten minutes, then takes you along the shore, where the birds are waking up.A break is planned facing the rising sun, with a hot drink. Nothing is timed: you paddle at your own pace
