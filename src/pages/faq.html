Frequently asked questions

An answer to every question before you leave. If yours is not here, write to us.

Book and cancel

  • How do I book an activity?

    Fill in the booking form: activity, date and number of participants. We check availability and confirm your place within 24 h, by email or WhatsApp. No online payment is required.

  • What is the cancellation policy?

    Cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before your outing. After that, we offer you another date.

  • Can I book for a group?

    Yes. Tell us the group size in the booking form and we adapt the boats, guides and schedule.

Safety and weather

  • Do I need to know how to swim?

    No. A life jacket is provided and worn throughout the activity, and a guide stays with you. Just tell us if you are uneasy in the water.

  • What happens in bad weather?

    Safety comes first. If wind, storms or heat make the outing risky, we reschedule it for another date or refund it. We warn you the day before at the latest, or the same morning if the weather changes.

  • Can children take part?

    Yes, most activities are open from age 6 when accompanied by an adult. The minimum age is shown on each activity page.

Prepare your outing

  • What should I bring?

    Sunscreen, a hat, water, and clothes that can get wet. For the Gourougou hike, add sturdy walking shoes.

  • Which languages do your guides speak?

    Our guides speak Arabic, French, Spanish and English.

  • How do I get to the meeting point?

    The exact address and a map link are sent in your confirmation. Nador-Al Aroui airport is about 25 minutes away, and we can arrange a transfer on request.
